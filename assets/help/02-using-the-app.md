# Using the app

## Plan your month

Tap **Plan** on Home (or **Edit plan** later). Enter:

- **Income**: take-home pay for the month, for example a salary of 150,000. Add more than one if you have them.
- **Fixed costs**: bills that don't change, like rent of 40,000.
- **Savings goal**: what you'll save first, for example 20,000.

The top of the screen shows **Spendable**: income minus fixed costs minus savings. You can split it across the four pillars, or tap **Split evenly**.

## Save first

After planning, Home asks you to put your savings aside. Move the money to a savings account, then tap **Done**. The ring on Home gets a red "saved" stamp.

## Log a spend

Tap **+**, type the amount on the number pad, tap a pillar, then **Save**. You can also add a note, change the date, choose how you paid, and tag how you feel about it (Worth it, Neutral or Regret).

To fix a mistake, tap the spend in **Spends** to edit it. Swipe it left, or long-press, to delete. There's always an **Undo**.

## Log a refund

Tap **+**, type the amount you got back, tap **Refund**, choose the pillar of the original spend, then **Save**. The refund lowers that pillar's total.

## Home

- **Left to spend** and how much **a day** keeps you on track. Tap **(?)** next to a number to see how it's worked out.
- The **savings ring** shows how your goal is going.
- **Pillar bars** fill as you spend. Amber means you're close to the budget, red means over. Tap a bar to see its spends.

## Weekly check-in

On the last days of each week, Home suggests a check-in. You can also open it from **Weekly check-in** under Recent. It shows the week by pillar and asks: are you on track? One thing to watch this week?

## Reflect at the end of the month

When a month ends, the reflection opens by itself once. It shows what you saved, asks the four Kakeibo questions, and lets you choose one improvement for next month. If you close it, your answers are kept and it waits as a card on Home.

## History and My stats

**History** shows what each month saved, a savings line, and spending by pillar. Tap a month to read its reflection. **My stats** shows your logging streak and habits.
