# Questions

## I started mid-month

Your first month runs from your month start day, so it may have begun before you installed the app. Plan with what's actually left: the income you still have this month, the fixed costs still to pay, and a smaller savings goal. From next month, plan the full month as usual.

If you're paid on a set day, change **Settings → Routine → Month starts on** to that day.

## I overspent a pillar

That's okay. The bar turns red and shows by how much. Overspending one pillar only matters if the month as a whole goes over, which then comes out of your savings. Try spending a little less in another pillar, and look at the reflection at the end of the month.

## I missed my savings goal

The reflection shows by how much, which pillar went furthest over, and suggests (never forces) a smaller goal or a smaller budget for that pillar next month. A missed month still teaches you something.

## How do I log a refund?

Tap **+**, enter the amount you got back, tap **Refund**, choose the pillar of the original spend, and **Save**. It lowers that pillar's total.

## I forgot my PIN

On the lock screen, tap **Forgot PIN?** and type your 12-character recovery code. You'll then set a new PIN and get a new code. If you turned on fingerprint unlock, that works too.

If you have neither the code nor fingerprint, the only way in is **Reset app**, which deletes everything on this phone. You can then restore your latest backup file.

## How do I move to a new phone?

1. On the old phone: **Settings → Data → Back up now**, and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone (Google Drive, email it to yourself, or a USB cable).
2. On the new phone: install Kakeibo, then **Settings → Data → Restore from a backup** and choose the file.

Restoring replaces everything on the new phone.

## I didn't do last month's reflection

That's fine. A new month starts by itself and you can keep logging. The reflection waits as a card on Home until you do it.

## Why does the projected saving look so low?

Early in the month one big spend can make the pace look worse than it is. The ring shows a projection from day 4. If you're over budget, it shows how much of your goal is still safe instead.

## Can I change the currency symbol?

Yes: **Settings → Currency format**. You can choose Rs, Rs., LKR, රු or ரூ, or type your own, and put it before or after the number. Your amounts stay in Sri Lankan Rupees.
