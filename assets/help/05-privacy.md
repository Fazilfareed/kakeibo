# Privacy and permissions

## Your data stays on this phone

Kakeibo has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere. My stats and every number in the app are worked out on the phone.

## App lock and the recovery code

**Settings → Security → App lock** asks for a 4 to 6 digit PIN when the app opens. The PIN is never stored, only a scrambled version of it, so no one can read it from the phone.

When you set a PIN you get a **recovery code** of 12 letters and numbers. Write it down and keep it away from the phone. It's the only way back in if you forget the PIN and can't use fingerprint. After 5 wrong tries the app makes you wait, longer each time.

## Why Kakeibo asks for permissions

- **Notifications**: only for the reminders you turn on: logging, the weekly check-in, and planning and reflecting. They're set on the phone.
- **Battery**: some phones stop apps' reminders to save battery. If reminders don't arrive, open **Android Settings → Apps → Kakeibo → Battery** and choose **No restrictions**.
- **Fingerprint**: only if you turn on fingerprint unlock.
- **A folder**: only the one you choose for automatic backups.

## Diagnostics

If something goes wrong, the app writes the error to a log on the phone. **Settings → Help → Export diagnostics** lets you share it if you want help. Nothing is sent unless you share it.
