Still open

  • Past reflections aren't viewable yet. FR-55 says a saved reflection can be read in History. History is built in M4, so the answers are stored but not shown anywhere yet.
  • Check-in reminder. Until reminders arrive in M5, Home nudges you on the last two days of each week. The check-in can always be opened from the button under Recent.
  • Mood chips behind the keypad. Still open from M2: on this phone's large font the third mood chip sits under the keypad in quick add.
  • Your phone has test data. November test figures and 4 spends from M2, plus the sample October with its finished reflection and one check-in. Clear it from Android Settings → Apps → Kakeibo → Storage.

Not covered by this run

  • The “Did I keep last month's improvement?” page on the phone. It only appears from the second reflection; a widget test covers it.
  • A real month changeover on the phone (the sample month stood in). The rollover is covered by unit tests.
  • The check-in nudge card on Home, since today is not one of the last two days of the week. Its date rule is simple and the week maths has unit tests.
  • Dark mode on the new screens, reduced motion, and a full TalkBack run.

Automated tests

  • flutter analyze: no issues.
  • flutter test: 61 passed, 11 new in M3.
  • Data: a closed month stays pending until its reflection is finished. The summary covers savings, goal, mood lists, the biggest overshoot and the suggested goal. A draft never puts the improvement on Home, finishing does. Totals freeze after completion and later edits are tracked (FR-58). Weeks are clipped to the month for Monday and Sunday starts. Check-ins save once per week and total the week.
  • Widgets: the reflection opens by itself and every answer reaches Finish; a reflection already opened waits as a card; the check-in shows the week and saves the note.
