Still open

  • Savings trend line. It needs at least two finished months, and the phone has one, so it was checked by a widget test only.
  • Automatic weekly backup. FR-73 (a weekly backup to a folder you choose, and a Home warning after 14 days) is planned for M5. This M4 build stores the last backup date ready for it.
  • Debug builds have the INTERNET permission. Flutter adds it to debug and profile builds for development. The release manifest has none, and the new packages added none. To be checked again on the signed release APK in M6.
  • Files left in your Downloads. kakeibo-backup-2026-11-04-1747.json (a real backup of the test data) and kakeibo-test-not-a-backup.json (the fake file used for T06). Both can be deleted.
  • Your phone has test data. The November test plan and spends and the sample October, now restored from the backup.

Not covered by this run

  • “Edited after close” on the phone: no spend in October was changed after its reflection. Covered by unit and widget tests.
  • Restoring a backup made by a newer version of the app. Covered by a unit test.
  • Sharing the backup through the share sheet (Save to phone was tested).
  • Dark mode on the new screens, reduced motion, and a full TalkBack run.

Automated tests

  • flutter analyze: no issues.
  • flutter test: 74 passed, 13 new in M4.
  • Data: History lists months newest first, with frozen totals after a reflection, edits after close, lifetime savings from closed months only, and a live update when a spend is added. A backup restores to exactly the same data and replaces rather than merges. Wrong, damaged and newer-version files are refused with nothing changed and no snapshot written. Only the newest 3 safety copies are kept. Delete-all puts back the default pillars, payment methods and settings. CSV cells that could run as spreadsheet formulas are escaped (FR-75), and refunds export as negative amounts.
  • Widgets: History shows lifetime savings, charts and month cards. A month shows its totals, “Edited after close” and its answers. Delete-all asks twice, and cancelling the second question deletes nothing.
