The method

A notebook habit, made easy.

Kakeibo (家計簿) was created in Japan in 1904 as a simple household account book. Writing each spend down, and stopping to think about it, is what helps people save. This app keeps that habit and does the sums for you.

計画

Plan

At the start of the month, enter your income, fixed costs and the savings you'll set aside first. The app shows what you can spend.

記録

Log

Each spend takes three taps: type the amount, pick one of the four pillars, and save. Add a note or a mood if you like.

反省

Reflect

At the end of the month, see what you saved and answer the four questions. Choose one thing to do better next month.

Four pillars

Every spend has a place.

When you're not sure which pillar, ask yourself: do I need it, or do I want it?

生活

Needs

Groceries, bus fare, electricity, medicine.

嗜好

Wants

Eating out, shopping, takeaway, things you enjoy.

文化

Culture

Books, a course, a concert, a museum.

予想外

Unexpected

Repairs, a doctor's visit, the surprises that always come.

Each pillar has its own budget, and its bar fills as you spend. Amber means close, red means over.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Left to spend, every day

See what's left this month and how much a day keeps you on track. Tap (?) to see how each number is worked out.

A savings ring

Your goal at a glance. It shows where you're heading at your current pace, and a red "saved" stamp once you've set the money aside.

Three-tap logging

Amount, pillar, save. Add a note, the date, how you paid, and whether it was worth it. Swipe to delete, with Undo.

Refunds

Money back? Record a refund against the original pillar, so its total goes down.

A monthly reflection

When a month ends, see what you saved and answer the four Kakeibo questions. Your one improvement appears on Home all month.

Weekly check-in

Once a week, see the week by pillar, and write one thing to watch next week.

History and My stats

Saved so far, a savings line by month, spending by pillar, your logging streak and your habits.

Gentle reminders

An evening nudge to log, a weekly check-in, and reminders to plan and reflect. Turn on only what you want.

PIN and fingerprint lock

Keep your spending private with a PIN or your fingerprint, with a recovery code in case you forget.

Backups you control

Back up to a folder once a week, or save a backup file any time. Restore it on a new phone.

Learn as you go

A short welcome tour, tips on each screen, and an offline Help Center with answers to common questions.

Sri Lankan Rupees

Amounts show as Rs 12,500.00, and you can change the symbol to LKR, Rs. or your own.

Screens

Calm, clear, and yours.

Light and dark themes, with large text and screen-reader support.

Privacy

Your money stays on your phone.

Kakeibo has no account, no ads and no analytics. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

  • No internet permission: the app cannot send your data.
  • Your PIN is stored only as a scrambled version, never in plain text.
  • Backups are files you save and share yourself. They aren't encrypted, so keep them private.
  • Only the folder you choose for backups is accessed.
